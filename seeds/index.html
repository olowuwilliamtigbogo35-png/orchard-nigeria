Every seed carries a colour of possibility.

Some arouse courage. Some awaken joy. Some give confidence. Some nurture growth. Some offer healing. Some invite reflection. Some spread wisdom. Some empower a person to rise. Some inspire a dream to begin. Some open the way to opportunity. Some restore dignity. Some rekindle hope. And some quietly transform what a life believes is possible.

And within every colour is a human possibility waiting to unfold. These literary meditations follow that unfolding: the quiet movement by which an act of service touches a life, awakens something within it, and becomes more than was first planted.
