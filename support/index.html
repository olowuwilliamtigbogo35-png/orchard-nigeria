The Work Before Us

A Vision Grows When It Is Shared

✥

The Orchard was created to preserve the deeper meaning of service: not simply what was done, but what those acts made possible in human lives.

To support this vision is to help ensure that important stories are remembered, that their lessons travel and that the possibilities they reveal continue to nourish generations yet to come.

We do not support a seed because we can already see the tree. We support it because we understand what may be lost if it is never given room to grow.
