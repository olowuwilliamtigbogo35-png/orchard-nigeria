The Last Person She Thought Of

✥

The Planting

✥

Recognising that quality healthcare depends not only on hospitals and equipment but also on the wellbeing of healthcare workers, Senator Oluremi Tinubu, through the Renewed Hope Initiative, launched the Scrubs and Crocs Initiative, distributing 60,000 branded scrubs and pairs of crocs to frontline health workers across Nigeria's six geopolitical zones.

The initiative honours those who spend their lives caring for others, affirming that when caregivers are supported, patients receive better care and communities become healthier.

The Story

✥

Every morning, long before the sun had begun to colour the sky, the nurse would rise quietly, tie back her hair, put on her worn uniform and make her way to the hospital.

By the time the wards began to fill, she was already moving from one bedside to another. She comforted frightened children whose tears softened only when they heard her gentle voice. She held the hands of anxious mothers waiting for reassuring news. She wiped away tears, welcomed newborn babies into the world and, when the day demanded it, whispered silent prayers beside the beds of those taking their final journey.

One patient would leave.

Another would arrive.

The hours passed almost unnoticed.

By evening, she had cared for dozens of people, yet as she walked home, the weight of the day settled into her tired feet. Her uniform had faded with years of washing. Her shoes, worn thin from countless journeys through hospital corridors, had long since surrendered their comfort.

Few people noticed.

To most, she was simply doing her job.

Then, one day, something unexpected happened.

Someone placed a neatly folded set of scrubs into her hands.

Beside it rested a brand new pair of crocs.

For a long moment, she said nothing.

She simply looked at them, gently running her fingers across the fabric before her eyes settled on the shoes she had never thought to replace.

Then, almost to herself, she whispered,

“They remembered us.”

It was such a simple gift.

Yet it carried a meaning far greater than the clothes themselves.

For the first time in a long while, she felt that someone had cared for the people who spent their lives caring for everyone else.

She smiled.

Not because of the uniform.

Not because of the shoes.

But because, without saying a single word, they carried a message her heart had longed to hear.

“We see you.”

The Vision Behind the Seed

✥

The belief was simple.

Healing begins with those who heal.

Every doctor.

Every nurse.

Every midwife.

Every community health worker.

They stand between illness and hope.

Supporting them is more than a gesture of appreciation.

It is an investment in every patient they will ever serve.

For a nation that cares for its caregivers strengthens the health of all.

The Harvest

✥

The harvest of this seed includes:

  • Distribution of 60,000 branded scrubs and crocs to frontline healthcare workers.
  • Improved morale among healthcare professionals.
  • Greater recognition of the contribution of health workers.
  • Stronger support for quality healthcare delivery.
  • Renewed appreciation for those who dedicate their lives to caring for others.

Legacy Reflection

✥

The hands that heal are often the least celebrated.

They work while others sleep.

They stand through long nights.

They carry hope from one bedside to the next.

Most never seek recognition.

Yet every healthy nation rests upon their quiet devotion.

The care they extend to others deserves to be returned to them.

For when caregivers are strengthened, healing reaches farther than medicine alone.
