A Look at
the Harvest

There is a law operating quietly through the universe: what is sown must, in its season, return as harvest.

Enter the Harvest →
✥

The true harvest of a seed is often found in what it becomes. A book may become knowledge. An opportunity may become confidence. A helping hand may become enterprise. A moment of care may restore dignity. What begins as a single act can travel through a life and return in forms far greater than its beginning.

This is why a harvest cannot be measured only by programmes completed, institutions established or numbers recorded. Its truest measure is found in what happened afterwards: in the child who discovered possibility, the woman who found the means to rise, the young person whose talent found expression, the family strengthened in a difficult season, and the lives that went on to strengthen other lives.
