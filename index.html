TheOrchard

✥

In this Orchard, we have set before you not merely a collection of stories, but a field of seeds and their harvests.

Some were planted in the hearts of children and grew into confidence. Some were placed in the hands of women and became enterprise and dignity. Some found their soil in classrooms, libraries, hospitals, farms, marketplaces and communities. Others were planted so quietly that their significance could only be understood with the passage of time.

For that is the mystery of a seed: what is small at the moment of planting may become immeasurable in the season of harvest.

The stories gathered here are literary meditations on that mystery. They look beyond programmes to people, beyond interventions to possibilities, and beyond the moment of service to what that service may continue to make possible.

So, set forth into this Orchard.Walk among its stories.
Discover its Seeds.
Consider their Harvests.

And perhaps, somewhere along the way, ask what may be the most important question the Orchard can leave with any of us:

What will grow
because we chose to plant?
